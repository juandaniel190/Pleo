Pleo · Q2 2026 GTM Review · 14 May 2026 03 / 05 · Three frictions

Three frictions are slowing the deals already in play — silence, slippage, and a contracting bottleneck

Each lives in a different part of the funnel and needs a different owner. Wrong-owner intervention kills the recovery.

  • Silence · 34 deals worth €1.34M with no AE activity in 30+ days
    Wise · €161K · 85 days silent is the largest single exposure. 6 of 16 reps logged zero activity in the last 4 weeks — and 5 of those 6 own at least one stale deal. Owner: UK Sales Lead. This is a sales motion failure, not a marketing one.
  • Slippage · close-date push-out rate jumped from 0% → 19% in four months
    9 deals worth €405K had their close date pushed in May alone. Reps are no longer committing to dates — so the weighted forecast moves week-to-week. Owner: VP Sales · tight close-date discipline in forecast calls.
  • Contracting · all 3 deals stuck >90 days at €110K weighted
    Money frozen at the finish line. This is a legal/procurement issue, not a sales issue — the wrong owner won't move it. Owner: Deal Desk + Legal escalation.
